Guided chart playback

Playback reveals a line chart in stages, or steps any other chart through a series of prepared views. A short note at each stop explains what changed.

Stops, notes, and timing

A checkpoint is a stop at a chosen horizontal value, even between measurements. Each can have a note. Here, from one stop to the next takes 1.8 seconds. The axes hold steady while the line grows, and the readout and Table show measurements that playback has reached.

Try it

Jump to the busiest day, then move over a reached point in either plot. Both plots show the shared position. Reaching a checkpoint also highlights its matching chart point through linked selection.

Step through chart states

Bar, pie, scatter, histogram, heatmap, and dot charts have no line to grow. Instead, each state checkpoint sets what the chart shows: which series are visible, which item is highlighted, the visible range, or the value of a linked control. The marks glide from one state to the next.

Try it

Press Play and follow the three stops. Watch the Show choice switch to Shares at the second stop. Press Reset to return the chart to its starting view.

Each checkpoint changes only what it names, and later stops keep earlier changes. Previous and Reset restore earlier views exactly, so readers can step back without losing their place.

When to reach for it

Use playback when readers benefit from seeing a few events in order. Use state checkpoints to guide readers through a few views of one chart. Give each stop one short note and let the chart show the change.

For every reader

Tab reaches the playback controls and the status announces each stop. Playback jumps straight to the stop when reduced motion is on. On a phone, use the checkpoint controls to move through the chart. The charts follow the page theme, and the notes and tables are readable with scripts off. Advance to the last checkpoint to print the full charts.
