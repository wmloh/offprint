SVG animations

SVG animations draw, fade, and move parts of a picture in timed steps. Readers can play, pause, step, or drag along the timeline to any moment.

See it

Try it

Press Play and watch the line draw, the heading label fade in, the dot move and the ring grow. Pause, then drag the timeline backwards. Scrubbing means dragging to any moment in the animation.

Four ways to change a picture

Draw
Reveal a line along its length to show a route.
Fade
Change how visible a shape or label is.
Move
Slide a shape to a new position.
Attribute
Change a property of a shape, such as the radius of the green ring.

SVG pictures are built from shapes, so they stay sharp when enlarged. A single step can combine several changes or stagger their starts. The caption beneath the controls changes with the current step. Previous and Next jump between steps.

Start when the figure comes into view

Scroll until about half of the next figure is visible. Keep the pointer outside it to see playback start on its own.

When to reach for it

Use an animation when the order or movement explains something a still picture would leave unclear. Keep the opening picture readable and put one observation in each step caption. Leave autoplay off when nearby prose needs the reader’s attention first.

For every reader

Use Tab to reach the buttons and timeline. The arrow keys move the focused timeline. The labels stay legible at narrow widths and use the page’s light or dark colours. Pause at the frame you want before printing. With scripts off, the drawings and their regular captions remain visible.
