Card panels and figure steps

Card panels let readers pick a card and read its details beside it. Figure steps change a picture one state at a time as the reader moves through a list.

See it

Try it

Choose Search, then Theme. With a card focused, move with Up or Down and press Enter to open its detail.

Card panels can start on a chosen card or use the first one. On a wide screen, long card lists and long details scroll separately so the reader can keep their place in either pane.

Cards beside a figure

Reading route

When to reach for it

Use grouped card panels for related options with explanations of their own. Use figure steps when the same diagram should change as readers move from one labelled state to another. Short labels leave space for the picture and details.

For every reader

In a card panel, Up, Down, Home and End move focus. Enter or Space selects the card. Figure steps select as soon as they receive focus. On a narrow screen, the panes stack. The highlight and labels identify the current choice in either theme. Print and pages opened with scripts off show all the written details.
