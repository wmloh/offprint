Card panels and figure steps

Card panels let readers pick a card and read its details beside it. Figure steps change a picture one state at a time as the reader moves through a list.

See it

Try it

Move your pointer over Search, then Theme. With a card focused, move with Up or Down and press Enter to open its detail.

A detail can hold anything a page can: tags, emphasis, lists, keys, callouts, and small figures. Card panels can start on a chosen card or use the first one. On a wide screen, long card lists and long details scroll separately so the reader can keep their place in either pane.

Numbers, tags, and search

Long card lists can use three optional extras. You can turn each one on by itself:

  • Numbers appear in a shaded circle on the left of each card and count through every group. You can give any card its own number, and later cards continue from it.
  • Tags sit on the right of a card. A tag can use one of the site's colour roles, such as On by default, or be a custom tag with its own colour and tooltip, such as Chart add-on.
  • Search adds a filter box above the list that matches card titles and their tags.

Try it

Type chart or add-on in the filter box. Groups with no matches disappear. The open detail stays where it is until you point at a card that is still showing. Press Down to move from the box into the list, and press Esc to clear the filter. Rest the pointer on a tag to read its tooltip.

Figure steps can use the same extras. The steps in the next section are numbered.

Cards beside a figure

Reading route

When to reach for it

Use grouped card panels for related options with explanations of their own. Use figure steps when the same diagram should change as readers move from one labelled state to another. Short labels leave space for the picture and details.

For every reader

Point at or tap a card to select it. Up, Down, Home and End move keyboard focus, and Enter or Space selects the focused card. Figure steps select as soon as they receive focus. A filter box has its own label, and a short message announces when nothing matches. Up from the first card returns to the box. On a narrow screen, the panes stack. Print and pages opened with scripts off show all the written details. Without scripts, tags sit under each card title, and numbers and the filter box are left out.
