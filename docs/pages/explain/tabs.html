Tabs

Tabs stack several panels in one place and show one at a time. Tabs that share a group switch together everywhere on the page.

Try it

Choose Explore in the upper set. The lower set jumps to Search palette. Then choose Section outline below and watch the upper set return to Read.

Starting tabs and groups

Each tab panel holds its own content. The first tab normally opens at the start. The author can choose a different starting tab, as in this set. Sets in the same linked selection group match equivalent choices even when their labels differ. A group can also remember a choice between pages.

When to reach for it

Use tabs for short alternatives that answer the same question, such as different ways to find a section. Put facts everyone needs in the surrounding text so they are visible whichever panel is open.

For every reader

Press Tab to reach the selected tab. Use Left and Right to move and select, or Home and End to jump to the ends. Press Tab again to continue into the selected panel. On narrow screens, tabs wrap onto additional rows. The underline and weight mark the choice in either theme.

With scripts off, all panel contents appear together. Print the selected panel, then switch and print again if you need another.
