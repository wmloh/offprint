Figure zoom and zoom tools

Figure zoom opens a picture or chart in a full-screen view and returns it to its place afterwards. Zoom tools add a magnifier, a laser pointer, pens, and a ruler for presenting.

Official name
Figure zoom · Zoom tools
Turned on
On request
Signal strength
Quiet (zooming) · Loud (pen, highlighter, and laser marks)
Works with
SVG charts, which gain a crosshair in the zoom view
Good for
Presenting and studying detailed figures

See it

Try it

Click the sketch, or focus it with Tab and press Enter or Space. Press 2 and move the lens over a label. Press Escape to close the enlarged view and return to your place on the page.

Which blocks can open

Images and figures in the reading area open automatically when Figure zoom is enabled. A page can also opt a plain block in or keep a figure in place. Try these two examples.

Opted-in block. Click or focus this reading tip to enlarge it. Use the outline to return to a section.

Tools and shortcut keys

These are the default keys. Site authors can change them for a site or page.

ToolKeyWhat it does
Cursor1Use the figure’s own controls as usual.
Magnifier2Move a circular lens over the figure; the pointer hides so the lens shows everything. It starts at 2.5×. Scroll or use [ and ] to adjust between 1.5× and 6×.
Laser pointer3Hold the mouse button and move to draw a red laser line. It slowly fades after you let go.
Pen4Drag to draw a thin line.
Highlighter5Drag to draw a wide, translucent mark.
Eraser7With Pen or Highlighter chosen, pick the eraser left of the swatches and drag over marks. Any mark it touches disappears whole. Press 7 again to go back to drawing.
Crosshair or ruler6Read chart coordinates under the pointer, or drag a ruler on a picture. Hold Shift to constrain the ruler’s direction.

Pen and Highlighter have red, blue, green, yellow and purple swatches. Each remembers its own colour while the view is open. Use Undo (Ctrl+Z or Cmd+Z) to remove the last mark or bring back what the eraser removed, or Clear ink for all marks. Closing the view clears the marks and measurements.

Measure on a chart

Try it

Open the chart and press 6. Move the pointer over the plotted line to read its coordinates. Try 6 on the sketch above to drag a ruler, which reports distance in picture units or image pixels.

When to reach for it

Use figure zoom when a chart’s labels or a diagram’s details need more room, or when several people are viewing the same screen. Use the laser for a quick cue and ink for marks you want to keep in view while discussing the figure.

For every reader

Tab moves through controls inside the enlarged view. Escape, the Close button or clicking outside the figure closes it and returns focus. The same live figure moves back to the page with its own settings, such as the chart’s table view. Drawings and the ruler use a pointer. The controls follow the theme, and the figure fits the available space.

Close the enlarged view before printing the page. With scripts off, the figures stay in the reading column.
