The signal ladder

Every DocForm feature pulls the eye with a different strength, from loud to quiet. This ladder ranks them so you can choose what to spend attention on.

Medium

Labels and supporting signals that guide without competing.

Quiet

Text and tools that stay out of the way until needed.
