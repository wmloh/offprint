Teach a concept

Explain one idea so a newcomer can follow it from first sight to full detail. This recipe combines formulas, explained terms, layered detail, and stepped figures.

Goal
Help a newcomer understand one idea
Combines
TeX math, tooltips, peek bubbles, sidenotes, figure steps, SVG animations, cross-references, citations
Reading time
About 10 minutes

The goal

Teach a reader how a page outline is built. By the end, they should be able to count the entries in a simple outline and tell where each entry belongs. An outline turns the headings on a page into a list you can follow (Visual hierarchy: guiding the eye).

Make room for a second question

Why does the smaller heading move to the right?

That extra space shows which section the smaller heading belongs to. Someone looking for “Find a section” can scan for “Read a page” first, then look underneath it.

Features used

  • Tooltips explain a term where it first matters.
  • Figure steps and SVG animations show how the picture changes.
  • TeX math expresses the pattern, and Cross-references and citations let readers check the picture and the source.
  • Sidenotes and peek bubbles give short and long asides their own space.
