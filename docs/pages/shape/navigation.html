Ways to move around

Page groups and outlines

A thin line and a heavier label mark the start of each page group. A group can start collapsed, with only its name showing. The group containing the page you’re reading opens so you can see your place. The outline lists the page’s first-level headings and highlights your current section as you scroll. On a wide screen, drag the sidebar divider to resize it, or reach the divider with Tab and use the arrow keys. Double-click the divider to reset it.

Sidebar modes

Shown
The page list stays open on the left.
Hidden
The page list tucks away. A numbered section rail beside the text lets you jump to headings.
Auto
Move the pointer to the far left edge to peek at the list. It tucks away when you move back into the page. After you pick a page from the list, it stays open on the new page until the pointer leaves it.

Try it

On a wide screen, use the navigation button in the control pill or press N to cycle through the three modes. In Hidden, point to a rail number or reach it with Tab to see the heading it leads to. OffPrint remembers your mode when browser storage is available.

Heading links and the pager

Point at a heading or reach its chain button with Tab to copy a link that opens the page there. The pager shows Previous and Next links at the bottom of a page, in the site’s reading order. Find this page’s pager at the bottom, or visit Colour, roles, and theme and use its Next link to come back.

A page can hide its pager, for example when it ends with its own set of choices. Authors can also leave individual headings out of the outline and section rail. The heading below remains visible in the text.

When to reach for it

Group pages when readers need to choose among several topics. Keep headings short so the outline works as a quick map. Use the pager when a reading order helps, and hide it on a page that ends with its own set of choices.

The rounded control bar also offers focus mode (F) to soften nearby text, and J and K to jump forward and back through headings. The theme and search buttons appear when those features are available.

For every reader

At phone width, the sidebar opens over the page as a drawer. Choose a link, tap outside it, or press Esc to close it. When scripts are off, use the ordinary links in the text to keep moving.
