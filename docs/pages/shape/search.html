What gets searched

Live page and offline site index

DocForm reads the text on the open page directly. For other pages, it looks in a prepared search index, a collection of page titles, headings, and text saved with the site. That lets it find matches across the site without the internet. With the search box empty, it lists pages in reading order. Once you type, a match needs to contain every word you entered, and headings and titles rank ahead of matches in ordinary paragraphs.

When pages change, the offline asset tool can rebuild the index. Until then, other-page results can still show older text. Search can find words on the open page even when the index is missing.

Excluded areas

Try it

Open search again and type quiet margin sample. The visible example does not appear in the results.

Search also skips hidden areas, navigation, most controls, and typeset formulas. A heading left out of the outline can still contribute words to the surrounding section’s results.

Other-page outlines and previews

Pause over Visual hierarchy: guiding the eye, or reach that link with Tab. The preview offers a title and an extract from the index. Find the same page under Design with hierarchy in the sidebar and open the arrow beside its name to see its indexed headings. You can jump straight to a heading from there.

Try it

Type squint test in the search box and choose The squint test under Visual hierarchy: guiding the eye. The result opens another page at the matching heading.

The index also supplies the Used on links in the Glossary, so you can go from a definition to a page that uses it.

When to reach for it

Use search to jump to a topic you can name. Use the sidebar when you want to see how topics are grouped. Clear, specific headings help readers recognize the right result, especially when several pages use the same word.

For every reader

Use the up and down arrow keys to move among results, Enter to open one, and Esc to close the search box. You can use the magnifying glass on a phone. With scripts off, the browser’s Find command can still look through the open page, and the links below still work.
