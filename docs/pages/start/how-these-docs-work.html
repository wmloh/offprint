Hover tips and cards

Try it

Rest the pointer over either underlined term, or reach it with Tab, to see its tooltip. Then press and hold visual hierarchy until the small ring fills and its hold-to-expand card opens. You can hold Enter or Space while the term has keyboard focus. Press Esc to close the card.

The glossary

The Glossary brings definitions together on one page. Use the letter links at the top to find a word. When a definition lists where it’s used, those links take you to examples in the docs.

Sidenotes

The opening sentence under a title tells you what you’ll get from the page.That opening is called the lead paragraph. This page’s lead tells you that you’ll learn to look up words and open extra explanations.

Try it

Find the small number after the sentence above. When there’s room, the sidenote sits beside the main text. Click the number to see the note briefly light up. On a narrow screen, rest the pointer on the number, tap it, or move to it with Tab to read the note there.

Peek bubbles

Where to put an extra explanation

Keep the idea readers need in the main text. A sidenote can explain a phrase in one short sentence. A peek bubble can hold a longer example that some readers will want to skip.

Try it

Scroll until the titled block above is near the middle of the screen. The peek bubble grows open in place. Choose its title to bring it into view, then rest the pointer on it, tap it, or move keyboard focus to it to read the text clearly. Keep scrolling to see it fold away.
