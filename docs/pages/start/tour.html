Tour the reading interface

Use the reading tools on this page to find your place, change what’s on screen, and jump to another section. On a phone or a narrow window, the page list opens over the text.

Reading time
About 10 minutes
You will
Use every reading tool at least once

The sidebar and outline

Try it

If the left panel is out of view, use the navigation button in the top-left bar to show it. Choose the arrow beside Design with hierarchy to fold its list of pages, then choose it again to unfold the list. Under Tour the reading interface, choose Search to jump to that section. Come back to this heading using the same list.

The left panel is the sidebar. Its page tree groups related pages. The headings under the current page form its outline. As you scroll, the outline highlights the heading you’re reading.

On a wide screen, drag the divider at the right edge of the sidebar to change its width. You can also press Tab until the divider has keyboard focus, then use the left or right arrow keys. Double-click the divider to reset the width.

Moving between pages

Try it

Rest the pointer over this heading or move to its link button with Tab. Choose the chain icon to copy a link that opens the page at this section. Paste it into a note to see what was copied. If your browser blocks copying, look for the section link in the address bar.

On a wide screen, use the sidebar button in the top-left control bar until the sidebar is set to hidden. The numbered section rail appears beside the text. Rest the pointer on a number or reach it with Tab to see its heading, then choose it to jump there.

At the bottom of this page, choose Previous to revisit the opening guide. Its Next link brings you back here. Together those links form the pager, which follows the site’s reading order.

The control pill

The rounded bar in the top-left corner is the control pill. It holds the reading controls used on this tour.

Try it

Scroll down a few sections and back up. Watch the ring fill and empty as your position changes. Then turn on focus mode with its button or F. Notice how text around the section you’re reading softens. Rest the pointer on a softened block to bring it into focus, then press F again for the regular view.

Sidebar modes

On a wide screen, click the sidebar button or press N to cycle through shown, hidden, and auto. In auto, move the pointer to the far left edge to reveal the sidebar and move back into the page to let it tuck away. Try all three, then leave it in the mode you like.

Theme switch

Choose the sun or moon button, or press T. The theme changes between a light background and a dark background. Switch again if you prefer the first appearance.

Search

Try it

Choose the magnifying glass in the control pill, or press S. Type sidebar modes and choose the matching heading on this page. You can also move through results with the arrow keys and press Enter to open one.

The box that opens over the page is the search palette. It shows matching headings and a little of the text around your search words. Press Esc to close it without choosing a result.

On a phone

Try it

Open this page on a phone or make your computer’s browser window narrow until the sidebar disappears. Choose the navigation button in the control pill. The sidebar opens over the page as a drawer.

Choose a heading to jump to it and close the drawer. Open it again and tap outside it, or press Esc, to close it while staying where you are.
