When to reach for it

Use inline math for a short expression the reader needs mid-sentence. Give a formula its own line when readers will stop to study it, and explain its symbols nearby.

For every reader

OffPrint includes KaTeX, the tool that draws the formulas, so math works offline. With scripts off, the original TeX notation stays visible. Put any essential explanation in the page text so it’s available in print and without opening a tooltip.
