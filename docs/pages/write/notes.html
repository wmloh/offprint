Sidenotes and peek bubbles

Sidenotes put small asides in the page margin, and peek bubbles keep optional detail folded until a reader wants it. Both keep extra explanation close to the text it supports.

Official name
Sidenotes · Peek bubbles
Turned on
Sidenotes On by default · Peek bubbles On request

Quiet
Works with

Good for

A note in the margin

An OffPrint page starts with a title and a short lead paragraph.The lead gives readers a reason to keep going. On this page, it tells you where you’ll find extra detail.

Try it

Find the small number at the end of the sentence. On a wide screen, look for the same number beside the reading column. Click the number in the sentence to make its note flash.

This numbered aside is a sidenote. OffPrint places it in the right margin, the space outside the reading column, when there’s enough room.

The note when space is tight

Try it

Hover over the number in the figure or reach it with Tab. If you narrow your browser window, the first note uses the same kind of card. OffPrint keeps notes in figures and other contained areas beside their marker even when the page margin is free.

An explanation that opens in place

Why the lead comes before the labelled fields

The lead tells you what you can learn here. Once you know why the page matters, the labelled fields give you quick facts such as which features are on by default.

Try it

Scroll toward the titled panel until it’s near the middle of the screen. It opens in the reading column. Point at it to clear the blur, or choose the title to bring it into view. Scroll on to see it fold away.

This is a peek bubble. It gives a longer aside its own space while keeping the page easy to scan.

A disclosure you open yourself

What a section heading tells you

A section heading names one topic. Short headings also make the outline easier to scan.

Try it

Scroll this panel past the centre of the screen, then choose its title. This disclosure, a block with a title that opens or closes its contents, has opted out of the peek effect. It stays in the reading column and opens only when you choose it.

When to reach for it

Give a short aside to a sidenote and a longer optional explanation to a peek bubble or disclosure. Put any idea everyone needs in the main text.

For every reader

Use Enter or Space on a bubble’s title to bring it into view. Focus or tap to clear the blur, and press Esc while focused on the bubble to close it. With scripts off, sidenotes read as asides in parentheses and peek bubbles work as ordinary disclosures.
