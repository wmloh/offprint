Choosing a JSON parser for the ingest service

We benchmarked six JSON parsers (made-up names) on a laptop and a server, with small and large inputs. The ingest service has two limits: a p99 latency of 5 ms per document and workers with 64 MB of memory. Use the chips and the header filters to see which parsers fit, and how they compare once the others are hidden.
