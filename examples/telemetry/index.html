A week of server telemetry

Three made-up servers report CPU load every ten minutes, so a week is just over 3,000 readings. At this length the two incidents and the maintenance window are hard to read. Hide the servers you don't need and zoom in on the hours that matter.

CPU load by server

Things to try:

  • Drag across the chart to zoom in on a stretch of time. Reset range zooms back out.
  • Click api-1 and api-2 in the legend to hide them. This chart rescales, so the y axis fits worker-1 on its own. The break on November 12 is the maintenance window, when no readings were sent.
  • From the keyboard, focus the chart and press + to zoom in on the reading under the readout, - to zoom out, and Shift with the arrow keys to move along. Table lists only the readings in the range.
  • Click an empty part of the plot to show the chart full screen for a talk. Press C for a crosshair that reads the time and load under the pointer, M for a magnifier (scroll to change it), L for a laser pointer, or P and H to draw. Esc closes the view and wipes the ink.

The outlined reading stays selected if you zoom away from it or hide its server. It shows again when it comes back into view.
